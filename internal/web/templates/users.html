A new password link lets someone set a new password, and using it logs them out everywhere else. Disabling someone logs them out and stops their API tokens. Their feed links keep working, so that friends on a group read aren't cut off.

Two-factor

Someone without it is sent to set it up before they can do anything else. Logins with single sign-on are left to the provider. Turning off someone's two-factor is for when they've lost their phone and their recovery codes.

Invites

What a user invites is always a user. A place is one use left on one of their links, so a link for three people takes three. Admins have no limit. Make links on the invites page, and find whose invites let who in with the invited by column.

Passwords

A character that isn't a letter is a digit, a symbol or a space. The kinds are lower-case letters, capitals, digits and anything else. This applies to passwords set from now on, not to ones people already have. Passwords on a common list, ones with the person's name in and ones of one repeated character are always refused.
